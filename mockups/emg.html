Why does training collapse?

Batch order. Fed in recording order, the model sees mostly rest and learns to predict rest for everything. The recurrent state was the other suspect, and it was not the cause.

What would this model cost on a prosthetic?

A prosthetic hand runs on a small battery-powered chip, so model size and compute per prediction decide what it can run. HGRN is a gated recurrent network with a linear state update. The LSTM is the usual baseline.
